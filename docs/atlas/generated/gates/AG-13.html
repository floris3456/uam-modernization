What are we proving?

Prove every portal action is purpose-bound, realm-isolated, current, audited transactionally, independently verifiable, and usable accessibly.

Why now?

Real roles and access cannot be invented during endpoint work and depend on server and lifecycle meaning.

What depends on it?

  • Governed administration and migration disposition workflows after approval.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Use T1 fictional prototypes to catalogue routes, capabilities, workflows, audit events, and accessibility risks after G0.

Work allowed

  • T1 capability vocabulary, route catalogue, wireframes, and audit/accessibility test models after G0.

Work prohibited

  • Create real organizational roles or permissions early.
  • Disclose before access audit or permit cross-realm/action leakage.

What must humans decide?

  • Define purpose, roles/capabilities, realm administration, approvals, JIT, break-glass, audit access, accessibility acceptance, and support ownership.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Complete route/action matrix, policy mutation, stale/revoked/JIT/approval/break-glass cases, transactional audit fault injection, external verification, keyboard and accessibility tests.

What evidence is required?

  • Approved capability model, route catalogue, decision/audit schemas, transaction traces, verifier output, accessibility report, and exception register.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on cross-realm/action leak, stale/revoked authority, effect without audit, disclosure before audit, undetected tamper, or inaccessible critical workflow.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Disable the affected route/capability, revoke temporary access, preserve audit evidence, repair atomically, notify named incident owners, and retest the whole route class.

What does passing unlock?

  • Governed administration and migration disposition workflows after approval.

What passing never authorizes

  • Organizational access policy invented by engineering.

Pass condition

Every route and action enforces current purpose and capability state, realm isolation, transactional audit and independent verification; critical workflows meet approved accessibility criteria.
