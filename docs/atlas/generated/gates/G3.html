What are we proving?

Prove every profile and history generation has stable identity and progress never skips or mixes records.

Why now?

Safe bytes are not useful if records can be assigned to the wrong source or skipped.

What depends on it?

  • Crash-safe endpoint progress and batching together with G4.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Exercise fictional replacement, regression, duplicate, rename, and cursor-boundary histories.

Work allowed

  • Fictional discovery and cursor models after G0; lab measurement after G2.

Work prohibited

  • Use path, profile name, or timestamp alone as source identity or cursor.
  • Silently retain progress across a discontinuity.

What must humans decide?

  • Approve supported discovery bounds and source-discontinuity handling.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Exact-recall suites for discovery bounds, rename/replacement, generation change, ID regression, ties, duplicates, crashes, and replays.

What evidence is required?

  • Source identity vectors, cursor ledger, actual-versus-oracle report, discontinuity traces, and cleanup.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on source, session, or realm mixing; path/name identity; timestamp cursor; retained discontinuity; or duplicate effect.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Quarantine the source generation, keep the prior checkpoint, reproduce from a fictional copy, and require an explicit reset path.

What does passing unlock?

  • Crash-safe endpoint progress and batching together with G4.

What passing never authorizes

  • Unbounded profile discovery.

Pass condition

All bounded discovery, continuity, native-ID cursor, and duplicate-effect cases match the independent truth ledger.
