What are we proving?

Prove the server acknowledges only durable custody and produces one realm-isolated effect despite retries, leases, crashes, and poison input.

Why now?

Endpoint deletion is unsafe until server custody has exact meaning.

What depends on it?

  • Database, capacity, lifecycle, and portal server proofs.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Build fictional inbox, receipt, lease, quarantine, and materialization models after G0.

Work allowed

  • Fictional relational state models and failure experiments.

Work prohibited

  • Acknowledge before durable custody.
  • Commit under a stale lease or expose one realm to another.

What must humans decide?

  • Choose service ownership, hosting boundary, durability objective, quarantine access, and incident response.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Crash/fault injection at every custody and lease boundary, retry storms, duplicate IDs, stale workers, poison bounds, quarantine, realm isolation, and recovery.

What evidence is required?

  • Protocol contracts, relational schema, transition ledger, transaction traces, lease tokens, quarantine bounds, and oracle comparison.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on receipt without bytes, stale lease commit, duplicate effect, cross-realm access, or unbounded poison handling.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Stop receipts for the affected partition, preserve fictional custody state, fence workers, quarantine poison, and replay from the last durable boundary.

What does passing unlock?

  • Database, capacity, lifecycle, and portal server proofs.

What passing never authorizes

  • A database product choice without experiments.

Pass condition

Receipts bind durable custody; fencing prevents stale commits; retries yield one ordinary effect; poison and realm behavior stay bounded and isolated.
