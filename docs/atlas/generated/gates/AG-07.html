AG-07 — Device identity and network

Accepted-baseline wording: device identity and enterprise network compatibility

Research: completeADR: not-createdImplementation: blockedGate: open
Owner
UNASSIGNED
Hard dependencies
AG-06
Proof claim
Enrollment, renewal, cloning, revocation, wrong-realm denial, proxy/VPN behavior, and direct mTLS fail safely.

What are we proving?

Prove each device has a unique realm-bound credential and works safely through the approved enterprise network paths.

Why now?

The server cannot trust endpoint claims without proven device identity and network behavior.

What depends on it?

  • Authenticated server ingestion and broader platform qualification.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Research certificate enrollment, TPM, cloning, proxy, VPN, and revocation; use fictional identities.

Work allowed

  • Fictional certificate and protocol models; isolated lab experiments after release proof.

Work prohibited

  • Use shared/exported production keys.
  • Trust payload realm claims or bypass TLS verification.

What must humans decide?

  • Choose certificate authority, enrollment ownership, TPM requirements, supported proxy/VPN paths, and incident response.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Enrollment, renewal, loss, clone race, revoke, wrong realm, duplicate device, proxy, VPN, offline, clock, and TLS interception tests.

What evidence is required?

  • Test certificate chain, device/release binding, network tuple, denial traces, revocation timing, cleanup, and residual risk.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on shared/exported key, payload-derived realm, revoked or duplicate authorization, clone winner, or TLS bypass.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Revoke test credentials, quarantine the duplicate identity, restore network policy, verify denial, and remove all lab certificates.

What does passing unlock?

  • Authenticated server ingestion and broader platform qualification.

What passing never authorizes

  • Production enrollment approval.

Pass condition

Unique realm-bound credentials, revocation, clone handling, network compatibility, and direct mTLS satisfy approved negative and recovery tests.
