What are we proving?

Measure database semantics, recovery, operations, and 6,000-device demand before selecting limits or a product.

Why now?

Research cannot decide numeric settings, capacity, or PostgreSQL versus SQL Server for the real workload.

What depends on it?

  • Measured operational settings and long-outage proof.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Define the simulator, workload envelopes, semantic corpus, objectives, and experiment protocol without choosing a winner.

Work allowed

  • Build a synthetic fleet simulator and identical database experiments after contracts stabilize.

Work prohibited

  • Choose a database from research preference alone.
  • Invent throughput, latency, retention, outage, or cost targets.

What must humans decide?

  • Set service objectives, cost/licensing boundary, staff/operations constraints, and acceptable headroom before selecting a database.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Semantic parity, backup/restore, failover, index/migration, 6,000-device steady load, reconnect storm, backlog drain, fairness, saturation, and broker break-even measurements.

What evidence is required?

  • Workload generator version and limits, hardware/software tuple, raw sanitized metrics, objective cards, cost/license assumptions, runbooks, and reproducible reports.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on semantic divergence, acknowledged loss, deleted visibility, generator saturation, backlog that cannot drain, starvation, or unknown objectives.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Mark the run invalid, preserve configs and measurements, restore the database fixture, verify generator headroom, and rerun without tuning away failed semantics.

What does passing unlock?

  • Measured operational settings and long-outage proof.

What passing never authorizes

  • Unmeasured production scale or budget approval.

Pass condition

At least the chosen candidate passes semantic and recovery parity and meets named demand, recovery, fairness, and headroom objectives on a reproducible measured tuple.
