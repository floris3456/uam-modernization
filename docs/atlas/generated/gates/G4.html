What are we proving?

Prove forbidden values are removed before IPC and ambiguous application matches fail closed.

Why now?

Privacy must be proven before acquired records can enter the durable pipeline.

What depends on it?

  • Privacy-safe events for the endpoint outbox proof.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Build transformation, matcher, Unicode, encoding, derivative, and canary cases using fictional G0 data.

Work allowed

  • Fictional transformations and canary scanners after G0.

Work prohibited

  • Invent production fields or purpose.
  • Allow tenant policy to broaden the product ceiling.
  • Guess an ambiguous match.

What must humans decide?

  • Define and approve the product privacy ceiling and tenant-narrowing rules.
  • Approve application ownership and ambiguous-match handling.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Property and adversarial tests across URL forms, encodings, aliases, ambiguity, derivatives, realms, policy versions, and every declared sink.

What evidence is required?

  • Versioned fictional rules, decision traces without forbidden values, canary matrix, scan report, and oracle comparison.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on forbidden value or derivative escape, guessed ambiguity, tenant broadening, cross-realm result, scanner miss, or mixed rule interpretation.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Disable the affected transformation release, preserve sanitized rule IDs and hashes, remove escaped test artifacts, and rerun all sinks.

What does passing unlock?

  • Privacy-safe events for the endpoint outbox proof.

What passing never authorizes

  • Unapproved production collection fields.

Pass condition

Every allowed result is minimized before IPC; every forbidden value or derivative is contained; ambiguity, broadening, and cross-realm cases fail closed.
