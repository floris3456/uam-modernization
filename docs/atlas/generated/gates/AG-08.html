What are we proving?

Prove support data is closed and privacy-safe, and qualify one exact Windows/browser/virtualization/security tuple at a time.

Why now?

Operations need safe evidence before a wider endpoint rollout can be considered.

What depends on it?

  • A supportable endpoint pilot boundary after later approvals.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Define fictional diagnostic schemas, cardinality budgets, support bundles, and a compatibility evidence template.

Work allowed

  • Fictional schema and support-bundle tests; one approved lab tuple after predecessor gates.

Work prohibited

  • Collect dumps, arbitrary files, commands, or high-cardinality identifiers.
  • Generalize one tuple to all Windows environments.

What must humans decide?

  • Approve diagnostic fields, temporary-access controls, support ownership, platform matrix, EDR coordination, and incident response.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Schema allowlist, privacy scans, cardinality bounds, expiry/revocation, cross-realm denial, sleep/RDP/RDS/FSLogix/Citrix/ARM64/EDR cases per exact tuple.

What evidence is required?

  • Exact tuple, structured logs, metric catalogue, bundle manifest, access audit, expiry proof, and unsupported list.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on a forbidden value, arbitrary field/command/file, dump, cross-realm access, expired permit still active, unbounded series, or stale/broad tuple claim.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Disable the diagnostic feature, revoke permits, delete bundles, scan sinks, and retract unsupported compatibility claims.

What does passing unlock?

  • A supportable endpoint pilot boundary after later approvals.

What passing never authorizes

  • Broad compatibility or support approval.

Pass condition

Closed diagnostic signals and temporary access pass privacy, isolation, expiry, and cardinality tests, and every compatibility claim is tied to one exact measured tuple.
