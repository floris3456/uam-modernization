AG-15 — Cutover and decommissioning

Accepted-baseline wording: cutover, read-only operation, trust removal, residual monitoring and decommissioning

Research: completeADR: not-createdImplementation: blockedGate: open
Owner
UNASSIGNED
Hard dependencies
AG-12, AG-13, AG-14
Proof claim
There is one unambiguous writer at every stage, rollback boundaries are explicit, legacy credentials and write paths are removed, and residual attempts are detected and handled.

What are we proving?

Transfer authority once, keep rollback safe, remove legacy trust in layers, observe residue, and decommission only after independent acceptance.

Why now?

It is the final proof after the replacement and the migration evidence are accepted.

What depends on it?

  • Independent approval to retire the legacy system when every acceptance record exists.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Draft fictional cutover, rollback, read-only, credential-removal, residual-monitoring, exception, and cleanup runbooks.

Work allowed

  • Runbook and fictional state-machine preparation; staged real execution only after all dependencies and approvals.

Work prohibited

  • Permit two writers, ambiguous authority, unsafe rollback, valid legacy trust, or decommissioning with missing owners or cleanup.

What must humans decide?

  • Approve cutover authority, maintenance and rollback boundaries, archive policy, exception expiry, residual observation period, trust removal, and independent final acceptance.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Authority-fence transitions, read-only enforcement, rollback rehearsal, credential and network trust removal, residual-write detection, exception expiry, archive validation, uninstall, and cleanup verification.

What evidence is required?

  • Signed authority state, exact fences and commands, before/after inventories, rollback evidence, credential revocations, residual observations, exception register, archive proof, and independent acceptance.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on two writers, ambiguous fence, unsafe rollback, valid legacy trust remaining, successful residual write, expired exception, or missing owner/cleanup.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Freeze authority changes, return to the last explicit safe fence when permitted, revoke exposed trust, restore read-only controls, investigate residue, and do not decommission.

What does passing unlock?

  • Independent approval to retire the legacy system when every acceptance record exists.

What passing never authorizes

  • Automatic decommissioning or deletion of legacy evidence.

Pass condition

Every stage has one accepted authority; rollback is safe within its declared fence; legacy writes and trust are removed; residual monitoring closes; exceptions expire; archive and cleanup are independently accepted.
