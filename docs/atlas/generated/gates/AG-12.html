Hard dependencies
AG-09, AG-10, AG-11
Proof claim
Tombstones and acknowledgements reconcile before reads resume, and no unsupported platform tuple is claimed.

What are we proving?

Prove deletion remains effective through backup/restore and replay, then qualify additional Windows environment profiles individually.

Why now?

Restore and later platform variants can reopen deleted data or break earlier invariants.

What depends on it?

  • Lifecycle-safe portal workflows and migration comparison.

Preparation that may happen earlier

  • Model tombstones, exports, backups, restore barriers, integration deletion, and later Windows fixtures.

Work allowed

  • Fictional lifecycle state machines; isolated restore and platform experiments after predecessors.

Work prohibited

  • Make restored data readable before reconciliation.
  • Invent retention or legal conclusions.
  • Claim an untested Windows tuple.

What must humans decide?

  • Approve retention/deletion policy, legal holds, export/integration obligations, restore authority, and extended platform priorities.

What must be measured with CLI evidence?

  • Delete/export/integration tombstones, backup, restore, fork/gap, acknowledged replay, pre-ready denial, and per-tuple Windows regression tests.

What evidence is required?

  • Lifecycle contracts, tombstone and ACK ledgers, backup/restore IDs, readiness barrier proof, visibility queries, and exact tuple reports.

What causes failure or hold?

Stop on deleted data visible, ACK missing or duplicated, tombstone gap/fork, pre-ready authority, or unsupported tuple claimed.

Failure, reproduction, cleanup, and recovery

  • Keep reads disabled, quarantine the restore, reconcile tombstone/ACK history, re-delete derivatives, and independently verify before readiness.

What does passing unlock?

  • Lifecycle-safe portal workflows and migration comparison.

What passing never authorizes

  • A production retention policy without human approval.

Pass condition

Deleted data stays unavailable across backup, restore, replay, export, and integrations; ACKs remain exact; reads wait for reconciliation; each later tuple passes its named profile.
